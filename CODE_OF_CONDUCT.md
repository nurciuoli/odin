# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment and
personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/nurciuoli). Reports are handled confidentially.
